    • Introduction

    The Philippines is home to some of the most stunning natural wonders in Southeast Asia. From clear water beaches to ancient buildings, here are five spots every traveler should see.

    Apo Island

    Apo Island is a tiny volcanic island off Negros Oriental, Philippines, globally renowned as a pioneering marine sanctuary and premier eco-tourism destination.

    Fort Santiago

    Fort Santiago is a 16th-century historic citadel and defense fortress located in Intramuros, Manila, at the mouth of the Pasig River.

    Kawasan Falls

    Kawasan Falls is a famous multi-layered waterfall system with striking turquoise-blue natural pools located in the jungle of Badian, Cebu, Philippines.

    Lake Kayangan

    Lake Kayangan is a stunning, crystal-clear freshwater lake on Coron Island in Palawan, Philippines, widely known as the cleanest lake in the country.

    Secret Lagoon

    Secret Lagoon in El Nido is a hidden, shallow emerald pool enclosed by towering karst limestone cliffs on Miniloc Island.
